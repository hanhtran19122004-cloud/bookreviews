Christopher Jude Starke

No Treaty for the Gray Zone

The New Cold War Without the Old Rules

How do great powers pressure each other without crossing into open war? Christopher Jude Starke examines how competition plays out through trade, finance, technology, cyber operations, and alliances. The book offers a way to connect developments that often appear in separate headlines, from restrictions on technology to pressure on trading partners.
