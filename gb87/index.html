Elizabeth Johns

The Nature Recovery Handbook

A Practical Guide for Citizens, Communities, and Local Groups Restoring Habitats and Protecting Biodiversity

What can a neighborhood group do to bring wildlife back to an overlooked patch of land? Elizabeth Johns starts with what is already there: the soil, the path rainwater takes, and the habitats nearby. Case stories and exercises help groups turn those observations into a plan for native planting, invasive species management, and monitoring. The guide also covers permissions, working with neighbors, and keeping the project going after the first volunteer day.
